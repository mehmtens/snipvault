# SnipVault brand guidelines

SnipVault is a secure, focused workspace for code and text. The identity should feel precise, dependable, and quietly confident—not playful or security-theatre heavy.

## Logo system

- **Primary lockup:** `internal/httpapi/web/logo-lockup.svg`
- **Symbol:** `internal/httpapi/web/logo-mark.svg`
- **Browser icon:** `internal/httpapi/web/favicon.svg`
- The four corner shapes represent a vault aperture; the diagonal seam represents both a code slash and a closing vault door.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to half the symbol height.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, outline, shadow, or place the logo on visually busy imagery.
- Minimum digital size: 120 px for the lockup and 24 px for the symbol.

## Color

- Vault navy: `#07151F` — primary background and dark logo detail
- Signal green: `#35E07B` — symbol, active state, and primary action
- Surface navy: `#111F2D` — cards and panels
- Cloud white: `#F8FAFC` — primary text
- Slate: `#A8B5C7` — supporting text

Signal green is reserved for meaning: brand recognition, successful state, focus, and the most important action.

## Typography

- Product and interface: IBM Plex Sans
- Code, labels, and technical metadata: JetBrains Mono
- Use compact, sentence-case copy. Prefer direct verbs and calm language.

## Voice

Clear, technical, and reassuring. SnipVault explains what happens without exaggeration. Avoid hacker clichés, fear-based security language, and unnecessary jargon.
